Frequently Asked Questions

Is my money safe with Qoondeeye? expand_more

Qoondeeye is a monitoring tool, not a wallet. We never have access to your actual funds. We only read the transaction confirmation messages you receive to help you visualize your spending habits.

How much does it cost? expand_more

Qoondeeye is currently free to download and use. If paid features are added in the future, they will be clearly explained inside the app before you choose to use them.

Will it drain my battery? expand_more

No. Qoondeeye only wakes up for a fraction of a second when you receive a financial SMS. It's designed to be extremely lightweight and energy-efficient, having zero impact on your daily battery life.

Does Qoondeeye read all my SMS messages? expand_more

No. SMS Auto Import is optional, and Qoondeeye is designed to detect transaction SMS patterns from supported providers. You can turn SMS Auto Import off anytime from Settings.

Is Qoondeeye a wallet or bank? expand_more

No. Qoondeeye is not a wallet, bank, or payment service. It does not hold, send, or receive money. It only helps you organize and understand your transaction records.

What if Qoondeeye misses a transaction? expand_more

Some providers may send new or unsupported SMS formats. If a transaction is missed, you can report the SMS format through support so we can improve detection in future updates.

Which devices are supported? expand_more

Qoondeeye is currently built for Android devices. SMS Auto Import depends on Android SMS permissions.
